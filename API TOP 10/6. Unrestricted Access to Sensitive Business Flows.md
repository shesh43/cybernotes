# API06 Unrestricted Access to Sensitive Business Flows /n
## **Sensitive Business Flow:** _Abuse a sensitive business process._ /n
This happens when an attacker can repeatedly or automatically abuse a sensitive business function without proper controls

### Impacts 
Sensitive Business Flows → Business/financial abuse
1. Financial/business abuse.
2. Automated abuse of legitimate functionality.
3. Loss of revenue or business reputation.

### How to Test
I test for Unrestricted Access to Sensitive Business Flows by identifying business processes that could cause abuse if an attacker automates or repeatedly performs them. I then check whether the application has controls to distinguish legitimate human use from automated or abusive activity

For example, sensitive flows could be:

- Creating multiple accounts
- Sending OTPs or password-reset requests
- Applying coupons or promotions
- Purchasing limited-stock items
- Posting votes, reviews, or comments
- Sending invitations or referrals
- Booking/canceling reservations

I would test whether I can **repeat or automate the flow excessively**, bypassing intended business restrictions.

example :
```
POST /api/coupon/apply
Authorization: Bearer <user-token>

{
  "coupon": "DISCOUNT50"
}
```

If the business rule says the coupon can be used **once per user**, but I can repeatedly invoke the flow and receive the benefit each time, that's a potential vulnerability.

### Important distinction

This is **not simply “no rate limiting.”**

The question is:

 **Can an attacker abuse a legitimate business function at a scale or frequency that the business intended to prevent?**

I identify sensitive business workflows, understand their intended business rules, and then test whether a user can automate, repeat, or manipulate those workflows to obtain an unintended business advantage because the application lacks appropriate anti-automation or business-flow controls.

### How to Prevent 
Sensitive business flows → Anti-automation + business rules
1. Implement **anti-automation controls**.
2. Enforce business rules server-side.
3. Detect and limit abnormal/repeated activity.
